# Moonfroggy Managed Hosting — Landing Page Copy

Privacy-first managed hosting for static sites, with built-in analytics and attribution.

## Value proposition

- **Static by default**: Astro + Islands + SolidJS, zero-JS baseline, edge CDN.
- **Privacy-first analytics**: self-hosted Umami, no third-party trackers, GDPR-ready.
- **Attribution proof-of-concept**: UTM session tokens + Umami custom events, no cookies or PII exports.
- **No Amazon, no ads, no surveillance**: aligned with the static-site and OpenAnalytics trends.

## Target buyer

Indie hackers, small studios, and privacy-first operators who want the performance of static hosting without handing their analytics to walled gardens.
